# Job Checkmark Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Job Checkmark ("the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

## Data the extension handles

Job Checkmark reads job listing content on LinkedIn and Indeed (such as job title, company name, salary, and location) in order to mark jobs you have applied to, display salary information, and provide filtering and search features. It also stores your preferences, such as your minimum salary and hidden locations.

## How your data is stored

All data is stored locally on your own device using your browser's built-in storage (chrome.storage.local). Your data never leaves your computer.

## Data we do not collect

Job Checkmark does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising. It has no servers and requires no account or sign-up.

## Permissions

The extension only runs on linkedin.com and indeed.com. It does not access any other websites or your browsing history.

## Data removal

You can remove all stored data at any time by removing jobs in the popup or by uninstalling the extension.
